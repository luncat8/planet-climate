## Your two runs found real bugs in what I shipped

First, the honest part: the batched page you ran had two defects, and your `30-05-00`/`30-05-01` pair is what proved them.

- **`ocean` = 0.001–0.029 ms at L7** is impossible for a 163,842-cell pass with ~50 dependent `texelFetch`es per cell, while `step` read **17.15 ms** where the whole ×10 frame is 5.99 ms — 10 steps cannot cost 171 ms. Two causes: `gl.finish()` does **not** force a batch of one small pass even on your GPU, and repeating one stepper alone drives the fields off the manifold, so every later pass was timed on garbage (that's the `closure` = 0.00/0.28).
- **Header column order was wrong**: I inserted the `tracers ms @64k` header before `mh.lastChild`, which is the trailing whitespace *text node* — so it landed after `n/batch` while the cells stayed in (tracers, tracers@B, n) order. Your `0.205 0.237 16` is 16 k = 0.205 ms, 64 k = 0.237 ms, n = 16. Both logs carry that note.

Fixed: every batch now ends in a 1 px forcing readback on **all** renderers (your data killed my "software-only" gate), each pass is re-timed from a `serializeState()` snapshot restored per batch with n ≤ 32, the calibration batch is no longer counted as a sample, and the header insertion uses `children[]`.
