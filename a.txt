> ## 9. Physical Conservation Laws and Advanced Inter-Layer Ocean Coupling
> 
> To ensure the planetary climate behaves as a closed, physically consistent system, the ocean model enforces strict conservation laws:
> 
> 1. **Mass / Volume Conservation:**
>    - Introduced a sea-surface height ($\eta$) field stored in a double-buffered auxiliary texture.
>    - The top layer thickness is $h_{top} = 200.0 + \eta$, and the deep layer thickness is $h_{deep} = 800.0 - \eta$. Total ocean depth $H_{total} = 1000$ m is strictly constant.
>    - Evolved $\eta$ via a discrete, conservative finite-volume continuity equation: $\frac{\partial \eta}{\partial t} = -\nabla \cdot (h_{top} \mathbf{u}_{top}) + \text{rain} - \text{evap}$, using upwind face thickness values.
>    - Prevents global volume drift with a high-precision mass-correction relaxer.
> 
> 2. **Momentum Conservation Between Layers:**
>    - Added a direct mechanical drag term resisting shear: $\mathbf{F}_{drag} = C_{drag} \cdot (\mathbf{u}_{top} - \mathbf{u}_{deep}) \cdot (1 + 0.5 |\mathbf{u}_{top} - \mathbf{u}_{deep}|)$.
>    - Acceleration changes are equal and opposite, weighted by respective layers' mass/thickness: $\mathbf{a}_{drag\_top} = -\mathbf{F}_{drag} / (\rho_0 h_{top})$ and $\mathbf{a}_{drag\_deep} = \mathbf{F}_{drag} / (\rho_0 h_{deep})$. Total oceanic momentum is exactly conserved.
> 
> 3. **Energy (Heat) and Salinity Conservation:**
>    - Tracks heat content as $C_p \rho_0 h T$ and salinity mass as $h S$.
>    - Evolved temperature and salinity in conservative flux-form: $Q^{new} = Q^{old} - \Delta t \cdot \nabla \cdot (h \mathbf{u} T) + \Delta t \cdot \nu \cdot \nabla^2(h T)$.
>    - Evolved vertical exchanges symmetrically and thickness-weighted: $\Delta T_{top} = H_{mix} \cdot w_{top}$ and $\Delta T_{deep} = -H_{mix} \cdot w_{deep}$, where weights $w_{top} = h_{deep} / (h_{top} + h_{deep})$ and $w_{deep} = h_{top} / (h_{top} + h_{deep})$ adapt dynamically. Net global heat is conserved.
>    - Evaporation removes water but leaves salt behind, naturally concentrating salinity under the updated $h_{top}$ thickness. Rain dilutes it similarly. No salt is ever created or destroyed.
> 
> 4. **Inter-Layer Coupling / Friction:**
>    - Integrates explicit mechanical shear friction during the dynamics step, which matches real-world oceanic viscosity at the interface of boundary layers.
>    - Added a new **Sea-surface height** rendering view mode (Mode 10) to let the user visualize real-time dynamic tides, piling-up of water, and gyres.
