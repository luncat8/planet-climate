frame ms = full frame (physics substeps + flow update + draw), draw ms = draw only (globe + tracers, no stepping). max fps is compute-limited; on a vsync display the GUI still caps at your refresh rate (typically 60). “sub@60” intuition: any config whose frame ms is under ~16.7 runs at a smooth 60. Explicit is the cheaper ocean integrator; implicit adds the Jacobi free-surface solve. Higher level ≈ 4× the cells per step; higher particle count scales the tracer draw.
