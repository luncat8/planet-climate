3. **Symmetric aquaplanet (no continents).** With no land/sea contrast and no topography there are no stationary highs/lows and no baroclinic **storm tracks** — so no *traveling* cyclones emerge. The equilibrium is zonal and time-invariant. This is physically expected, not a bug.
4. **Weak symmetry-break noise** (`noise = 0.02`) → little to spawn transient disturbances.
